Getting started

How do I create a session?
Sign in, tap to create a new session, set the date, courts and how you want players picked (Pegboard, Skill-based or Manual), then share it with your members.
What is Pegboard mode?
It's the classic club pegboard, made digital. Players "peg on" when they arrive, the queue updates live, and courts fill fairly in the right order as games finish — so everyone can see who's next without crowding the board.
How do players check in / peg on?
Players open the session and check in from their phone. You can also run NextFour on a tablet by the courts (Terminal mode) so players check themselves in there.

Clubs

How do I join or create a club?
You can create a club from the app, or join an existing one. New clubs are reviewed before they go live.
How do I become a member of a club?
You're added to a club automatically when you take part in one of its sessions as a player.

Subscriptions & billing

What do the paid plans unlock?
NextFour is free to use for core club nights. Paid plans unlock additional features such as Pegboard and Skill-based picking, Terminal (courtside tablet) mode, and more.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play and are managed there:
  • iOS: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → NextFour.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → NextFour.
How do I restore a purchase?
Open NextFour on the device signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account and use "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen.
