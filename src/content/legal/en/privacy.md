This Privacy Policy explains how NextFour ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares your personal data when you use the NextFour mobile application and related services (the "Service"). NextFour is operated from the United Kingdom and complies with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 1. Who we are

NextFour is a mobile application for racket sport clubs and players, enabling session management, court rotation, social features, and club administration. For any privacy-related enquiry, you can reach us at [privacy@nextfour.app](mailto:privacy@nextfour.app).

## 2. Data we collect

We collect only the data needed to operate the Service:

- **Account information**: name, nickname, email address, and (where you provide them) profile photo, gender, age band, and the sports you play with your skill level. Some older accounts also hold a date of birth.
- **Authentication data**: when you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique identifier from the provider.
- **Location**: the home area you choose by searching for a place. We store the place you pick (its coordinates and address details) so we can show sessions and clubs near you. The app does not use your device's GPS. We suggest choosing your town or area rather than your home address.
- **Club & session data**: clubs you belong to, sessions you join, check-ins, availability poll answers, match results, statistics, pairings, and queue position.
- **Skill ratings**: we calculate a rating for each sport and format from your match results, to help balance games. Ratings are not shown publicly.
- **Subscription status**: your subscription tier (Free, Performance, Pro, or Club) is managed by RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
- **Device information**: a push notification token, and for terminal/kiosk activations, a device name, device identifier, and last-seen timestamp.
- **Advertising data**: the free tier shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob receives your device's advertising identifier and IP address, and we pass it the sports you play so ads are relevant. Whether ads are personalised depends on the choice you make in the consent prompt, which you can change later (see section 3).
- **Usage and diagnostic data**: app events (such as signing up or joining a session) through Firebase Analytics and our own event log, and crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase data is not linked to your name; our own event log is linked to your account.

The camera is used only when you take a profile or club photo or scan a kiosk QR code. We do not access your contacts, microphone, or device location.

## 3. Lawful basis

We process personal data under the following UK GDPR lawful bases:

- **Contract**: to provide the Service you've signed up for.
- **Legitimate interests**: to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, understand how the Service is used, fix crashes, improve features, and calculate skill ratings.
- **Consent**: for personalised advertising and push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time. To change your ad choice, go to Profile → Privacy Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.

## 4. How we use your data

- To create and manage your account.
- To show sessions and clubs near you.
- To run live sessions, court rotations, queues, and matches, and to balance games using skill ratings.
- To display your club memberships and social interactions.
- To process subscriptions via RevenueCat.
- To send service-related notifications (e.g. session check-ins).
- To show ads in the free tier.
- To understand usage, diagnose bugs, and maintain the integrity of the Service.

## 5. Sharing & sub-processors

We share data only with the sub-processors needed to run the Service:

- **Supabase**: hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. Data is stored in EU regions where available.
- **RevenueCat**: handles subscription state.
- **Apple and Google**: authentication providers (only if you choose social sign-in).
- **Google Firebase**: usage analytics, crash reports, app configuration, and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- **Apple Push Notification Service**: push notifications on iOS.
- **Google AdMob**: advertising in the free tier, as described in section 2.
- **LocationIQ**: receives the place names you search for, to find locations.
- **OpenFreeMap**: provides map images; it receives your IP address and the map area you view.
- **Resend**: sends our service emails.

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Apart from Google AdMob as described above, we do not share data with advertisers.

## 6. Other users and club members

Your name, nickname, profile photo, sports, skill level, and the location you have chosen can be seen by other NextFour users, for example in session lists and player search. Profile photos are stored at a public web address. When you join a club or session, your statistics, queue position, and pairings are visible to other members of that club or session. This is necessary for the Service to function. Club hosts and deputies may see additional information such as your check-in history within their club.

## 7. Shadow profiles

Hosts may add "guest" players to a session without those players holding a NextFour account. These shadow profiles store only a name and (optionally) email and gender so the player can be referenced in the session. Shadow profiles do not have logins, are not used for analytics, and can be deleted at the host's request.

## 8. Club and session listings from public sources

To help players find somewhere to play, some club and session listings in NextFour are created by us rather than by the club. We compile them from publicly available sources such as league, county, club and venue websites. A listing shows the club's name, venue, day and time, and a link to its source. It does not show anyone's personal contact details.

Where a source publishes a contact address for a club, we may keep it privately so that we can contact the club about its listing. It is not shown in the app. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: helping players find clubs and keeping listings accurate.

If you run a club and want its listing corrected or removed, or you object to us holding a contact address, email [clubs@nextfour.app](mailto:clubs@nextfour.app). A club can also claim its listing in the app to manage it directly.

## 9. Data retention

We retain your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law (e.g. financial records). Data retained for legal reasons is restricted from further processing.

## 10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Request a portable copy of your data.
- Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the lawful basis.
- Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/).

To exercise any of these rights, email [privacy@nextfour.app](mailto:privacy@nextfour.app). See also the [Account & Data Deletion](/delete-account.html) page.

## 11. Security

Data is stored encrypted at rest by Supabase and transmitted over HTTPS. Database access is controlled by row-level security policies. We restrict administrative access and do not log raw passwords (authentication is handled by Supabase Auth or social providers).

## 12. Children

NextFour is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you tell us you are under 18, we turn off Firebase Analytics for you, stop passing your sports to our ad provider, and ask Google to apply its protections for teenagers to any ads you see. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so we can delete it.

## 13. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the UK or EEA (for example to US-based sub-processors), it is protected by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or an equivalent safeguard.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

## 15. Contact

For any privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact: [privacy@nextfour.app](mailto:privacy@nextfour.app)
